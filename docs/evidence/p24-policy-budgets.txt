# P24 — per-market action budgets (GAP-029) and the policy fingerprint (GAP-024, first half)

**Date:** 2026-10-13 · **Module:** `crates/sentinel-core/src/policy.rs` (additive)
**Tests:** `crates/sentinel-core/tests/policy_budget_adversarial.rs` (9)

## The findings this addresses

Straight from the enterprise evaluation's own component audit:

- **[P-1]** "No per-market caps or velocity limits. One global
  `MAX_ORDER_SIZE_USD` and `MAX_DAILY_ACTIONS`; a correlated multi-position
  cascade could burn the daily cap on the first market. Per-market +
  aggregate-velocity budgets belong here (GAP-029)."
- **[P-2]** "No policy versioning in the gate itself. Policy *changes* aren't
  hash-committed into the audit journal as first-class entries with
  before/after diffs. For institutional buyers, 'who changed the caps at 03:14
  UTC and what were they before' must be a journal query (GAP-024)."

## Design constraint: the gate is frozen

`PolicyConfig`, `DayState` and `PolicyEngine::evaluate` are normative under
`P05` §3: check precedence, exact deny reasons, and the Red+Reflex approval
asymmetry are pinned by adversarial tests. Adding required fields to those
structs would ripple through 13 construction sites and risk the frozen
semantics, so this slice is **additive**: new types that a caller consults
*around* the gate.

## What was built

### GAP-029 — `BudgetLimits` + `BudgetLedger`

- `BudgetLimits` carries per-market overrides for the per-action notional
  ceiling and the daily action cap, plus an optional **book-wide** daily
  ceiling. `order_cap(market)` and `daily_cap(market)` resolve the effective
  value.
- `BudgetLedger` tracks actions per market and across the book, with a pure
  `check()` (a planner can rank candidate slices without spending budget on
  the ones it rejects) and an explicit `record()` commit.

**The semantic distinction the tests pin down:** `global_daily_actions` is the
*per-market default*, not a book ceiling. One market can spend the whole
global cap by itself; that is precisely the [P-1] hole. Closing it needs
`max_book_daily_actions`, and `a_correlated_cascade_cannot_burn_the_book_on_one_market`
reproduces the hole and then closes it.

**Adoption-safety property, asserted:** with empty override maps the limits
reproduce the global values exactly for every market, so adopting the ledger
cannot change the frozen gate's outcome for any existing configuration.

### GAP-024 first half — `policy_fingerprint`

A stable 64-hex identity for a `PolicyConfig`, using the same length-prefixed,
field-ordered encoding discipline as the P23 inference commitment (plain
concatenation would let two different policies collide). Adopted at the one
point that makes it immediately useful: the **strategy intent journal entry**
now carries `policy_fingerprint` alongside the serialized policy, so the
policy in force is part of the hash-chained record and a change is visible by
diffing up the chain.

## Honest scope statement

- **Landed and adopted:** the fingerprint (visible in every strategy intent
  journal entry from now on).
- **Landed with tests, enforcement NOT yet wired:** the budget ledger. Its
  consumer is either the cascade planner (GAP-007), which orders slices and
  needs to know the remaining budget before it starts, or a daemon config
  surface for per-market caps (new env vars + three `policy_config()`
  construction sites). Neither is in this slice, so the ledger is a library
  API today, not an enforced limit. Stating that plainly rather than ticking
  GAP-029 off.

## Reproduce

```bash
cargo test -p sentinel-core --test policy_budget_adversarial
```

Coverage: 9 tests including the behaviour-preservation property, the [P-1]
hole reproduction and closure, purity of `check`, non-recording of refused
actions, day reset, fingerprint determinism, per-field sensitivity and
encoding injectivity.

[2.3] Field-mapping caveat (why the fallback was NOT lifted beyond "candidate"):
  Event inputs are exchange-native ids and scaled integers, not the address/string
  forms the frozen entities need. From the vendor ABI:
    MakerOrderFilledV2 inputs: perpId, accountId, orderId, pricePNS, lotLNS, feeCNS,
      lockedBalanceCNS, amountCNS, balanceCNS, builderId, builderFeeCNS
    PositionLiquidated inputs: perpId, posAccountId, positionType, markPricePNS,
      liqPricePNS, liqLotLNS, posLotLNS, deltaPnlCNS, fundingCNS, posAmountCNS,
      posDepositCNS, accAmountCNS, accBalanceCNS, onOrderBook
    TakerOrderFilledV2 inputs: entryPricePNS, collatPricePNS, pnlPricePNS, lotLNS,
      feeCNS, amountCNS, balanceCNS, builderId, builderFeeCNS  (no perp/account ids!)
  → mapping `PerpFill.account` / `Liquidation.account` (String) requires the
    accountId → address relation (e.g. the exchange's AccountCreated(address,uint256)),
    and PNS/LNS/CNS fixed-point scales must be decoded into decimal strings.
    TakerOrderFilledV2 alone cannot populate the frozen fields; a correct design
    needs the (maker+taker) pair or on-contract id resolution per tx.

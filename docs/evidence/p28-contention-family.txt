# GAP-007 slice 4 — the contention family, and the regression it exposed

**Date:** 2026-10-13 · **Files:** `tests/scenarios-contention/*.json` (6),
`tools/scenarios/generate_contention.py`

## Why this family exists

Slice 3 measured 0.00% and traced it to the scenario set: the daily action
budget was never scarce (max 4 executed orders against a cap of 10), so every
allocation mechanism was provably inert. The KPI had no witness. This family is
that witness.

**Construction.** Real 1-minute ETH and BTC closes from the P21 tape
(`docs/evidence/p21-perpl-real-candles.json`), real return distributions.

- **24 warmup bars** of real returns. This matters: the earlier contention
  attempt failed because the first single-position action landed inside the
  tracker's 20-bucket warmup, so any reserve computed there was zero by
  construction. The warmup is what makes the measurement meaningful rather than
  degenerate.
- **12 correlated stress cycles** per scenario: both legs take a real
  tail-quantile down move amplified 3x, a partial recovery, then another down
  move, so positions keep re-entering the danger bands instead of being trimmed
  once and staying out.
- **`max_daily_actions = 3`**, which binds: base executes 3.0 orders per
  scenario and the cap is reached.

Validated: timestamps strictly increasing, every mark positive.

## The measurement

| run | capital saved | liq avoided | violations | orders |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| no cascade | $50.18 | 6 | 0 | 18 |
| `--cascade` | **-$263.00** | 6 | 0 | 12 |
| delta | **-$313.18 (-624%)** | 0 | 0 | -6 |

The mechanism engages on every scenario: **6 of 6 record portfolio budget
refusals**, and the order count drops from 18 to 12.

## It made things worse, and the reason is a design flaw I introduced

The reserve holds actions back from the reflex path. But the cascade planner
**only reorders** — it never emits its own reductions. So the actions the
reserve withheld are not transferred to the cascade; they are simply lost. Six
fewer reductions across the family, in a book whose positions each need
trimming repeatedly, converts a small gain into a large loss.

Stated as a rule: **a reserve without a corresponding commit is pure negative.**
Withholding budget from the path that acts, on behalf of a path that does not
act, removes protection and buys nothing. The intent/commit split has to be
completed — the cascade must *emit* the reductions it reserved for — before the
reserve can be anything other than a loss.

The `liquidations_avoided` column stays at 6 in both runs, consistent with the
documented property that sentinel-side liquidation accounting is formula-only
(P13 §5) and does not react to this mechanism. `capital_saved_usd` is the
column that carries the signal.

## Containment

`--cascade` defaults to **off**, so nothing shipped regresses: every frozen
result, the P13 suite and the P21 realistic suite all still measure exactly what
they measured before. The regression exists only under the experimental flag.
Enabling it by default would be wrong until the commit half exists.

The valuable output is the instrument, not a percentage. Before this slice the
gap was unmeasurable; now a change to the portfolio layer produces a number
with a sign, on a family whose cap genuinely binds, in 0.2 seconds.
